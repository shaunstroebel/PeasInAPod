History

Founded in 2023 in Durban by Sarah Smith, Pawsitive Pet Academy was created to bridge the knowledge gap in animal care, training, nutrition, and emergency response. Hundreds of pet owners and aspiring animal care professionals have graduated from our programmes.

Vision

To be South Africa's leading pet care educational institute, promoting compassionate, science-based animal welfare practices across all communities.

Mission

To empower pet owners and aspiring professionals with high-quality practical training, positive reinforcement techniques, and career advancement opportunities in the pet care industry.

Goals

  • Deliver accessible 6-week and 6-month hands-on practical training courses.
  • Foster entrepreneurship and create job opportunities within the regional pet care sector.
  • Improve community pet health, emergency preparedness, and overall animal well-being.
